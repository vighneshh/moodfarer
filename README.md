## What is Moodfarer?

Moodfarer is a personal well-being app for your phone or computer. Each day you answer a short, guided check-in about how you've been feeling. Over time, the app turns those answers into a simple picture of your ups and downs, so you can notice patterns you'd otherwise miss.

Alongside the check-in, Moodfarer includes small weekly habits ("Rewirements") that research links to feeling better, like gratitude, exercise, and connecting with others. You can track both in one place and see how they move together.

It's designed to feel easy and unhurried: one question at a time, large tap targets, and nothing flashy.

## Who is it for?

- People who want to keep a regular eye on their mood without a complicated journal.
- Anyone who has been asked to track their mood between appointments with a therapist or doctor.
- People who like building small, steady habits.

## What you can do

### Take a daily check-in
Answer 25 short questions, one at a time, about your thoughts, feelings, relationships, and physical well-being. Each answer is on a simple five-point scale from *Not at all* to *Extremely*. It takes just a few minutes, and you can go back to change an answer before you finish.

The questions come from the **Burns Depression Checklist**, a well-known self-assessment developed by Dr. David Burns.

### See your score in plain language
When you finish, you get a total score with a clear label (from *No depression* to *Extreme depression*) and a comparison with your previous check-in, so you can see whether things are trending up or down.

### Follow your trend over time
- A **trend chart** on your home screen shows your recent scores at a glance.
- **History** lists every check-in you've done. Open any day to see your answers, edit them, or delete the entry.
- **Insights** shows which areas are hardest for you, your best and hardest days, and how consistently you've been checking in.
- Missed a day? You can **log a past day** whenever you like.

### Build small weekly habits (Rewirements)
Eight simple practices, each with a week-at-a-glance tracker you tap to fill in:

1. Use your signature strengths
2. Savoring
3. Daily gratitude journal
4. Random acts of kindness
5. Social connection
6. Exercise
7. Sleep
8. Gratitude letter and visit

Several include a place to jot a quick note about your day. Your home screen shows how many of your habits you've completed this week, next to your check-in, so you can see the full picture.

The Rewirements are inspired by the *Science of Well-Being* course workbook.

### Stay on track
- **Reminders** by email (and optionally browser notifications) if you haven't checked in yet, at a time you choose.
- **Light and dark mode**, so it's comfortable to use at any time of day.
- **Export your data** to Excel or CSV whenever you want, for example to share with a therapist.

### Your account
Sign up with your email or with Google. You can change your name, email, or password, reset a forgotten password, and delete your account and all of your data at any time.

## Your privacy

Your check-ins belong to you. Everything you enter is tied to your own account and is not visible to other users. You can export or permanently delete your data at any time from the app.

## Important: please read

Moodfarer is a **self-tracking tool**. It is **not** a medical device and it does **not** diagnose, treat, or replace care from a qualified professional. Your score is a snapshot of how you've been feeling, not a diagnosis.

If you're struggling, please talk to someone you trust or a healthcare professional. If you are having thoughts of harming yourself, help is available right now. In the US, you can call or text **988** (Suicide & Crisis Lifeline). If you're elsewhere, contact your local crisis line or emergency number.

## How a typical day looks

1. Open Moodfarer and tap **Start Assessment**.
2. Answer the questions one by one. Your progress bar fills as you go.
3. See your score and how it compares with last time.
4. Tick off any weekly habits you did today.
5. Done. Come back tomorrow.

## For the curious

Moodfarer is a web app that works in any modern browser and is designed phone-first. If you'd like to run it yourself or see how it's built, see the [Developer Setup Guide](docs/DEVELOPER_SETUP.md).
